Welcome to China

Discover the beauty of ancient traditions, amazing food, and unforgettable destinations.

Why

This site exists to help visitors understand why China is such an important and exciting place to explore, from its traditions and cuisine to its breathtaking landmarks.

Expert

  • China has over 1.4 billion people, making it the most populous country in the world.
  • The Great Wall stretches for more than 21,000 kilometers in total.
  • China is home to more UNESCO World Heritage Sites than any other country.

History

China’s story stretches back thousands of years, beginning with early dynasties that built cities, developed writing, and shaped one of the world’s oldest continuous civilizations. Today, that long history still influences the country’s art, language, and traditions.
